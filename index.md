## 2. Information Handled by the App

### A. Local Storage on Your Device
All your personal app data is stored locally on your device using your device’s internal storage. This includes:
* **Subscriptions & Playlists**: Podcasts you subscribe to and custom playlists you create.
* **Playback History & Queue**: Episode playback progress, history, and play queue positions.
* **Downloaded Audio**: Episode audio files downloaded for offline playback.
* **App Preferences**: Audio preferences, playback speeds, sleep timer settings, and theme choices.

This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it (e.g., via OPML export).

### B. Network Requests to Podcast Publishers
When you search for podcasts, update feeds, or stream/download podcast episodes, the App makes direct HTTP/HTTPS connections to third-party RSS feeds and podcast hosting servers.
* When connecting to these servers, standard internet protocol requests occur, which may include your device's IP address and basic HTTP header data (such as the app User-Agent string).
* These requests are handled directly by the third-party podcast hosting providers, subject to their respective privacy policies.

### C. Integrated Third-Party Services
Depending on features you use, the App may interface with the following services:
* **YouTube**: For video podcast playback using embedded YouTube players. Your interactions with YouTube content are governed by [YouTube’s Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and[...]
* **Google Cast / Chromecast & DLNA**: For streaming audio to local devices (smart TVs, Cast receivers). Device discovery and media control take place exclusively over your local Wi-Fi network.

## 3. Data Collection, Tracking & Analytics
* **No User Identification**: We do not assign unique tracking identifiers to you or your device.
* **No Third-Party Ad Networks**: We do not bundle third-party advertising SDKs or tracking networks into the App.
* **No Analytics SDKs**: We do not track your personal usage habits or export telemetry to remote servers.

---

## 4. Permissions Requested by the App
The App requests specific Android system permissions strictly for function execution:
* **Internet (`INTERNET`)**: Required to fetch RSS feeds, stream podcast audio, and search for podcasts.
* **Network State (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`)**: Required to check network connectivity before downloading or casting content over local Wi-Fi.
* **Foreground Service / Media Playback (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**: Required to continue playing audio in the background and show playback controls in the notification shade.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all your data is stored locally on your device:
* You have complete control over your data.
* You can clear all cached files, downloads, and app settings at any time via your device’s **Settings > Apps > PodPlay > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Cache**.
* Uninstalling the App permanently removes all stored app data from your device.
